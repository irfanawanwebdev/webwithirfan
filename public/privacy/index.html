Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies and runs no ad trackers. The only personal data I receive is what you type into the contact form or send me yourself by email, phone, or WhatsApp. I use it to reply to you, and nothing else.

Who I am

This site is run by Irfan Awan (WebWithIrfan), a frontend and WordPress developer based in Multan, Pakistan. For anything related to this policy, email info@webwithirfan.com.

What I collect

When you send the contact form, I receive what you enter:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to add it
  • Project type, budget range, and your message

If you contact me directly by email, phone, or WhatsApp, I receive whatever you send through that channel. The site itself does not collect anything behind the scenes: no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no fingerprinting.

How I use it

I use your details to reply to your inquiry, discuss and scope your project, and work with you if we go ahead. I do not sell your data, share it for marketing, or add you to any mailing list.

Services that handle your data

A few third-party services process data on my behalf:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form submissions to my inbox (their privacy policy). Web3Forms states that it does not store your form data after delivery.
  • Vercel hosts this site. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address (their privacy policy).
  • Email providers store the messages I receive, the same way any email conversation is stored.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) applies its own terms if you choose to message me there.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not use local storage for tracking. If that ever changes, for example if I add privacy-friendly analytics, this page will be updated first.

How long I keep it

Messages live in my email inbox for as long as they are useful for our conversation or an ongoing project, or as long as the law requires. If you want them gone sooner, just ask.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time for a copy of the personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email info@webwithirfan.com and I will sort it out, normally within a few days.

Children

This site offers professional web development services and is not directed at children under 16. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Significant changes will be noted at the top of the page.
